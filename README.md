Download the app here: [Open the Releases page](https://github.com/iloveranni/relictool-of-nightreign/releases)

Generate better NIGHTREIGN loadouts from your existing relics based on the effects you need.

1. Download the app ZIP, extract **all** files, and run `Relic Tool.exe`.
2. Game saves and co-op saves load automatically. Select your save and character slot.
3. Choose your character, add effects, set “Required” or “Wanted,” and wait for results.

The UserData folder contains your save copies and personal settings. Do not share it with others.

Contributors: yiyi, Jia, Cinderella小辛, and other players of the Night Region.
